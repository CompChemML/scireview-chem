## Scientific status

The review did not locate a dedicated Na2NbOF5 MLIP/MLMD study, but absence from the captured search is not proof of non-existence. The primary-paper audit is provisional because the full paper PDF was not supplied. User-provided numerical details are clearly separated from independently verified facts.

The defensible surviving question concerns trajectory-length and finite-size convergence across O/F disorder realizations, together with collective-versus-tracer Na transport across temperature. It does not claim that prior AIMD ignored disorder or that MLMD will automatically explain the experiment–calculation discrepancy.
